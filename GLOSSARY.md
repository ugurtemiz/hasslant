# myVAILLANT home

The electricity a home's heat generator draws while heating, for the current day, as shown by the myVAILLANT app.

## Language

**myVAILLANT account**:
The email, password, country, and brand a person uses to open the myVAILLANT app.
_Avoid_: user, login, gateway

**Home**:
A dwelling registered on a myVAILLANT account.
_Avoid_: system, installation

**Heat generator**:
A device the home marks as its primary heat generator, or as a secondary heat generator.
_Avoid_: hydraulic station, indoor unit, outdoor unit, companion device

**Closed hour**:
An hour of the home's calendar day that has already ended.
_Avoid_: the hour in progress, a live sample

**Consumed electrical energy**:
Electricity a heat generator drew from the supply, in watt-hours.
_Avoid_: energy consumption, power, heat generated, earned environment energy

**Heating**:
The operation mode in which the heat generator supplies space heat.
_Avoid_: hot water, cooling, the heating system

**Day total**:
The sum of consumed electrical energy for heating across the home's heat generators, over the closed hours of the current calendar day that have a figure.
_Avoid_: lifetime total, month total, live power
